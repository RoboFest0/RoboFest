ABOUT ROBOFEST

ROBOFEST is a robotics-focused event that brings together creativity, engineering, and innovation. It provides a platform for students to explore robotics, develop technical skills, solve real-world challenges, and turn ideas into action.

Organised by ROBOTiX Club at NIT Raipur, ROBOFEST encourages collaboration, experimentation, and hands-on learning through the exciting world of robotics.
